Description

Nugent Auctioneers, 045 865 555, www.nugents.ie are delighted to present to the market this charming four-bedroom detached bungalow, ideally situated in Killalish Lower and enjoying a dramatic mountain backdrop with uninterrupted views of Keadeen Mountain.

The property offers spacious and well-proportioned accommodation, comprising a kitchen, utility room, large dining area and bright sitting room. There is also a family bathroom and four generous bedrooms, with the master bedroom benefiting from its own ensuite. Offering an excellent opportunity for the new owner to put their own stamp on the property, this home combines spacious accommodation with a peaceful and tranquil rural setting.

Located along the L7269, the property provides easy access to both the N81 and R747, making it an ideal location for commuters. Kiltegan village is approximately 4km away, while Baltinglass Town is just 6km, offering a wide range of amenities including schools, supermarkets, pubs, restaurants, sports clubs and more. For outdoor and hiking enthusiasts, the property is within easy reach of the stunning Wicklow Mountains National Park, offering an abundance of scenic walking trails opportunities to enjoy the surrounding natural beauty.
